About TN FOR BUS

An open, community-powered bus directory for Tamil Nadu. Built by riders, for riders — with clear rules about what we are and what we are not.

Community reported No ad trackers Independent project

What this is

TN FOR BUS is an independent transit directory where commuters record real-world bus routes, the stops in between, passing times and fares. Timetables in many towns are hard to find or go out of date quickly; rider observations keep the picture practical and current.

Every route is tied to the person who added it. Other riders can suggest corrections, and route owners review them before they go live. That keeps the community accountable without needing an official transit authority.

Independent service & legal disclaimer

TN FOR BUS is not affiliated with, sponsored by, authorised by, or endorsed by:

  • Tamil Nadu State Transport Corporation (TNSTC)
  • State Express Transport Corporation (SETC)
  • Metropolitan Transport Corporation (MTC, Chennai)
  • Any department of the Government of Tamil Nadu or Government of India

Bus names, numbers, routes, timings and fares are submitted by volunteer riders or derived from public community observations. TN FOR BUS does not sell tickets, collect fares for any operator, or claim official authority. Always confirm departure details at the bus stand.

Privacy policy

We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the service.

  • No ad trackers. We do not run third-party advertising or sell personal data.
  • Location stays local. “Use my location” is processed in your browser to centre the map and find nearby stops. We do not store your coordinates or track your movements.
  • Account details. If you sign in, we store your name, email, chosen username, a hashed password (never plain text) and, for Google sign-in, your Google profile ID and avatar URL.
  • Session cookies. We use HTTP-only session cookies only to keep you signed in.
  • Contributions are public. Bus routes, stops, timings and fares you submit are shown publicly. Your email is never shown on public route pages.

Community guidelines

  • Add only routes and timings you have seen or travelled yourself.
  • List stops in travel order and name them the way they appear on the board.
  • No spam, offensive content or deliberately false information.
  • Spot a mistake? Use Suggest an edit on the route instead of adding a duplicate bus.
  • Route owners review suggestions; repeated bad-faith edits can lead to account removal.

Maps & data

Basemaps come from OpenStreetMap contributors. Route lines and place search use public OSRM and Nominatim services. Data is stored on managed PostgreSQL and photos, when enabled, on object storage.

This project is not affiliated with OpenStreetMap, OSRM or Nominatim.
