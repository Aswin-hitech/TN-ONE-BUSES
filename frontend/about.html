About TN FOR BUS

TN FOR BUS is an open, community-powered transit directory created to help commuters, travelers, and residents across Tamil Nadu find real-world bus schedules, routes, and intermediate spot timings.

Transit timetables in many regions are hard to access or quickly become outdated. By enabling verified commuters to contribute observed passing times and stops, TN FOR BUS brings transparent, practical transit information to everyone.

Privacy Policy

We believe in strict data minimization and user privacy:

  • No Tracking Cookies: We do not use third-party advertising trackers or sell your personal data.
  • Location Privacy: When you use the "My Location" feature, your coordinates are processed strictly inside your browser to display nearby bus stops on the map. We never record, track, or sell your physical movements.
  • Account Information: If you create an account to contribute bus routes, we securely store your chosen username, email, and password hash (encrypted using industry-standard salted hashing).
  • Session Cookies: We use secure, HTTP-only session cookies strictly for maintaining authenticated sessions.

Community Guidelines & Terms

  • Please only submit accurate, real-world bus timings and routes that you have observed or experienced.
  • Do not submit spam, offensive content, or false information.
  • If you notice an error in a route, use the Edit Route feature to suggest a correction.
